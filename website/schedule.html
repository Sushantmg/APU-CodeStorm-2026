Venue

Where it happens

All sessions run inside Block C of the APU Cyberjaya campus. The Lecture Theatre hosts keynotes and demo day, while the Innovation Lab is the 24-hour build floor on Day 1 and Day 2.

  • Level 2 Lecture Theatre 1 – keynotes, judging, awards
  • Level 3 Innovation Lab – build floor, mentor desks, charging stations
  • Level 1 Cafeteria – meals, snacks and hydration station
