Challenge tracks

Six problems worth 48 hours

Each track is sponsored by an industry partner and judged by a panel of academics plus practitioners.

Track 01

AI & Data

Build a responsible AI assistant, a predictive model or a data-visualisation tool that solves a real campus problem.

Track 02

FinTech

Wallets, budgeting, micro-investing or fraud detection prototypes for students living on a semester budget.

Track 03

HealthTech

Wellness tracking, mental-health support or accessibility tools that improve student life inside and outside the classroom.

Track 04

Sustainability

Energy, waste and recycling solutions that move APU closer to a greener campus.

Track 05

Games & XR

Playable web games, extended-reality experiences and interactive storytelling built from scratch.

Track 06

Open Innovation

Anything goes: developer tools, education tech, social impact - if it is useful and demoable, it belongs here.
