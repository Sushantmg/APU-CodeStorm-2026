Activities & Competitions

Everything you can enter, watch or volunteer for across the 48 hours.

Track browser

Pick a challenge track

Switch between tracks to read the brief, deliverables and judging weightings.

Track 01 · AI & Data

Brief: build a responsible AI or analytics solution for a genuine campus pain point - timetable clashes, facility booking, academic advising or mental-health signals. Pre-trained models and public APIs are welcome, provided the team can explain their data sources.

Deliverable
Working demo + 3-minute pitch deck

Judging
Innovation 40% · Technical 30% · UX 20% · Impact 10%

Mentor
Data science panel from DataPulse

Track 02 · FinTech

Brief: design a money tool for students - budgeting, split bills, micro-savings, scholarship tracking or scam detection. Teams must include a threat model explaining how the prototype protects user data.

Deliverable
Prototype + security note

Judging
Feasibility 35% · Security 30% · UX 25% · Impact 10%

Mentor
FinTech engineering desk from Nexlify

Track 03 · HealthTech

Brief: tackle student wellbeing - sleep, stress, posture, nutrition or accessibility. Prototypes must be tested with at least five real users and report what changed after feedback.

Deliverable
Prototype + usability test report

Judging
Impact 35% · Evidence 30% · UX 25% · Innovation 10%

Mentor
Clinical UX panel from CloudNine

Track 04 · Sustainability

Brief: reduce the campus footprint - energy dashboards, food-waste tracking, recycling incentives or low-carbon travel planning. Real measurements beat estimates, so bring data if you have it.

Deliverable
Prototype + impact projection

Judging
Impact 40% · Feasibility 30% · UX 20% · Innovation 10%

Mentor
Green-tech desk from Quantum Labs

Track 05 · Games & XR

Brief: ship a playable experience in the browser or on a phone - a web game, an AR overlay, an interactive story or a XR lab demo. Fun is judged, but so is polish: crash-free playthroughs are required.

Deliverable
Playable build + design document

Judging
Fun 35% · Craft 30% · Originality 25% · Stability 10%

Mentor
Creative coding desk from PixelWorks

Track 06 · Open Innovation

Brief: the wildcard track. Developer tools, education technology, accessibility utilities, social-impact platforms - anything that solves a stated problem for a stated audience.

Deliverable
Working demo + problem statement

Judging
Problem fit 40% · Execution 30% · Demo 20% · Originality 10%

Mentor
Rotating panel from ByteForge
