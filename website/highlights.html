Highlights & Past Events

Editions

How CodeStorm grew

2023

The launch edition

32 teams, one track, a borrowed lecture theatre and a printed poster. The problem statement sheet went out an hour late, which is exactly why the schedule on this site is versioned.

Winner: StudyBuddy - a study-group matcher that later became a student society tool.

View archive photos →

2024

The crossover year

74 teams and the first all-female winning team, taking the Sustainability track with a food-waste tracker that is still deployed in the campus cafeteria.

Winner: PlateShare - kitchen-level waste logging with a weekly public dashboard.

View archive photos →

2025

The 100-team milestone

102 teams across four tracks, 212 survey responses and a submission server that nearly collapsed at 11:52 on the final morning - hence the second server in 2026.

Winner: AccessMap, an indoor navigation aid for wheelchair users on campus.
