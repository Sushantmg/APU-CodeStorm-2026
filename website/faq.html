Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not here, the committee replies to the contact form within two working days.

General

About the event

What is APU CodeStorm 2026?

It is a fictional 48-hour interfaculty hackathon created for the CT053-3-1 web design assignment. In the world of this site, teams of three to four students build working prototypes across six challenge tracks, mentored by industry engineers and judged on demo day.

Who can take part?

Any student currently enrolled at APU, from any faculty and any intake. Teams need three or four members, and at least one member must be in their first or second year. Solo participants can join the team-formation mixer on Friday morning.

Does it cost anything?

No. Registration, meals, merch and workshop materials are covered by the sponsor pool. Teams only cover their own transport if they are commuting from outside Cyberjaya.

Do I need to be a programmer?

No. Strong teams mix developers with designers, writers, business students and domain experts. The UI Design Sprint and Lightning Talk Battle are also open to students who do not write code at all.

Registration

Signing up

How do I register my team?

Fill in the registration form once, with the captain as the submitter. Add the other members in section 3. You will receive a team code by e-mail within two working days, and you can edit your roster from the participant dashboard until 27 November.

Can I change teams after registering?

Yes, once. Send the request through the contact form before 9 March and the committee will move you. After confirmations go out, rosters are locked so judging sheets stay accurate.

What if my team is missing a member?

Register anyway. Unfilled seats are matched during the formation mixer at 10:30 on Friday, where solo participants pitch themselves to teams that need help.

During the event

The 48 hours

Do we have to stay overnight?

No. The build floor is open around the clock, but overnight stays are optional and the campus closes the atrium between 01:00 and 06:00. Welfare staff are on call throughout, and a quiet room is available 24 hours.

What can we use from outside sources?

Open-source libraries, public APIs, pre-trained models, stock media and starter templates are allowed when credited in the build log. Code written before the kickoff must be declared; undeclared outside code counts as plagiarism.

What do we submit?

A repository link, the build log, a slide deck and a demo video under 200 MB, all uploaded through the participant dashboard before the code freeze at 12:00 on Sunday 6 December.

Judging & prizes

Scoring

How is the winner decided?

Each track is scored on the published rubric - innovation, technical execution, user experience, impact and pitch quality - by a panel of two academics and one industry judge. The Grand Champion is the highest-scoring track winner, and ties are broken by the People's Choice vote.

What are the prizes?

A RM 20,000 pool split across six track winners, plus the Grand Champion, People's Choice and the UI Design Sprint. Prizes include cash vouchers, internship interviews with partner companies and one year of developer tooling credits.

Will we get feedback if we lose?

Yes. Every team receives the judges' written score sheets by e-mail within five working days, including the marks lost on each rubric line.

Logistics

Venue, food and welfare

Where is the event held?

Block C of the APU Cyberjaya campus - Lecture Theatre 1 for keynotes and judging, the Level 3 Innovation Lab for building, and the Level 1 cafeteria for meals. See the venue map.

Is food provided?

Three meals, two coffee breaks and a midnight snack each day. Dietary requirements are collected on the registration form, and halal and vegetarian options are always available.

How do I report a problem?

Use the contact form, find the welfare desk in the Innovation Lab, or e-mail codestorm@apu.edu.my. Reports are confidential and answered within 24 hours during the event.

Still stuck? Send the committee a message and we will answer within two working days.
