Blog 14 September 2026

How to scope a 48-hour project in one hour

Our technical lead's checklist: cut the database, mock the data, pick one user role, define the single happy path, and only then decide what delight you can afford. Teams that follow this order finish; teams that do not spend Sunday morning rewriting migrations.
